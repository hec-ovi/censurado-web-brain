# Draft the institutional piece in the most formal register

Write the full body now, from the official source and your ledger. This is the fallback to
the MOST FORMAL house voice: there is no persona and no byline personality to embody. Write
institutional prose, objective and neutral, the way the newsroom itself would report an
official act.

- **Third person, always.** Never the first person ("yo"), and never a narrator with a
  personal stake. The piece reports what the source announced, not what a writer feels about
  it.
- **Objective and neutral.** State the facts and let them carry the piece. No opinion, no
  slant, no framing device, no humor, none of the persona idiosyncrasy the signed lanes use.
  A right-vs-left position has NO place here; this lane manufactures no angle.
- **Minimal gerunds.** Use as few `-ando` / `-iendo` forms as possible; prefer finite verbs.

Build it in layers: open with the self-contained summary of the official fact and its
concrete consequence (hecho before reacción), develop the detail, close with the full
context. Each layer is new and dense; never restate an earlier one.

**Say each idea once.** A caveat or a framing note is stated ONE time, in a single short
line, and never repeated. Do not stack three, four, or five synonyms for the same point, and
do not let it reappear in every paragraph. Any point the piece already made is advanced,
never echoed. This is a straight, institutional voice: it writes with no disclaimer, no
"opinión" or "análisis" label, and never names a byline.

Name every source as plain text in the prose ("según la Municipalidad", "según el comunicado
oficial"); do NOT add links in the body (no Markdown links). Be exact with names, titles, offices,
dates, and figures;
accompany every cifra with its source and its date. Ground each claim in the ledger; invent
no fact, no source, and no quote.

This is a provided-image lane: the picture is PROVIDED, not generated. Do NOT run the `image`
verb and do NOT invent a hero. If a second visual belongs mid-body, it is another provided
`/media/...` path (or a real http/https URL) with plain Markdown `![descripción](url)`. Where
your prose names an official post or an earlier Censurado piece, drop its marker inline where
it comes up, each on its own line: `{{tweet:<id>}}`, `{{video:<id-or-url>}}`,
`{{relacionado:<older-slug>}}`.

No AI-slop tells: no "en el mundo actual", no "es importante notar", no hollow both-sides
hedging, no three adjectives standing in for a fact. No em or en dashes anywhere (commas,
periods, parentheses, or a mid-sentence colon do that work), no "no es X, es Y" inversion or
aphorism built on a negation, no candor tics ("la verdad es que", "seamos honestos"), no
thing (a market, a law, a country) given feelings or will. Write the complete piece: there is
no length limit and no placeholders.
