# Accents and entities: the Spanish orthography check

One focused pass over the whole piece for correct Spanish and correct names. This is where
the accents rule lives, so do it carefully.

**Accents (critical).** Read the title, description, body, AND the topic tags.
Every word carries its accents (á, é, í, ó, ú, ü), its ñ where the word needs it, and every
question and exclamation carries its opening ¿ or ¡. Fix any stripped accent, missing ñ, or
missing opening mark. "programacion", "numero", "publico", "analisis", "economia" are
WRONG; write "programación", "número", "público", "análisis", "economía". Keeping an English
technical term in English (model names, "open source", "benchmark") is never a licence to
drop a Spanish accent anywhere else.

**Entities.** Every proper-noun entity the piece is about (people, organizations, places) is
named correctly and spelled as the sources have it, and each central one will be tagged as a
topic (`javier milei`, `diego santilli`, `donald trump`, `openai`), lowercase and accented
where Spanish requires. An entity tag is what builds that entity's topic page over time, so
name the protagonists, not only abstract themes.
