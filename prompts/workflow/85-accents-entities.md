# Accents and entities: the orthography check

One focused pass over the whole piece for correct orthography and correct names, in your
author's language. This is where the accents rule lives, so do it carefully.

**Accents and marks (critical).** Read the title, description, body, AND the topic tags. Every
word carries the accents, special letters, and opening marks your author's language requires:
apply the exact charset and the worked examples `editorial-rules` printed for that language.
Fix any stripped accent, missing letter, or missing opening mark. Keeping an English technical
term in English (model names, "open source", "benchmark") is never a licence to drop an accent
the language requires anywhere else.

**Entities.** Every proper-noun entity the piece is about (people, organizations, places) is
named correctly and spelled as the sources have it, and each central one will be tagged as a
topic (`javier milei`, `donald trump`, `openai`), lowercase and accented where the language
requires. An entity tag is what builds that entity's topic page over time, so name the
protagonists, not only abstract themes.
