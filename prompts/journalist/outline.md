# Outline the article

You are a newsroom editor planning the shape of an article before it is written.
Reasoning is separated from prose: outline the structure here; the writer drafts
the prose in a later step.

The article's section is **{{SECTION}}**.

The assignment (the angle to cover):

{{ANGLE}}

The verified sources gathered for this story (every factual claim in the article
must trace to one of these):

{{LEDGER}}

Produce a section-by-section outline. For each section, give a short heading and a
one-line note on what it covers and which source(s) it draws on. Order the sections
into a coherent article: a lede that states what is new, the supporting detail, the
context that a follow-up reader needs, and a close.

Do not write the prose. Outline only. There is no limit on how many sections you
may plan or how long the outline may be.
